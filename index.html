Telegram Secretary

A private, owner-operated Telegram assistant for personal scheduling and incoming email notifications.

This integration is being configured for a single owner. It is not a public account-connection service. This website contains product information only; it does not collect sign-ins, messages, credentials or payment information.

Calendar

The assistant checks the owner's primary Google Calendar for a specific proposed time. Telegram contacts may receive a polite availability answer. Event names, locations, attendees and the reason for being busy are not disclosed to contacts.

Contacts can provide meeting details, including voice messages. The assistant prepares a proposal. Only the owner can approve adding an event, after reviewing its details. Email invitations are not sent.

Email

The planned connection reads incoming Gmail messages and shows private owner notifications. Labels distinguish mail arriving from different source mailboxes. It does not send emails or change mailbox contents. Email data is not submitted to AI models.

Google access is granted through Google's own consent screen and can be revoked from the owner's Google Account. No Google password is given to the assistant.

Privacy policyUse and limitations

Support: contact the operator through the existing private conversation with the Telegram assistant. Access to its controls is restricted to the configured owner.
