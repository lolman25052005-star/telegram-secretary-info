Telegram Secretary

Use and limitations

Telegram Secretary is privately operated personal software. This page does not offer public sign-up, a subscription or paid services.

The owner controls Google authorization and event creation. A free/busy answer describes potential calendar availability at the time of checking; it is not acceptance of a meeting or a reservation. Calendar contents can change after a check. The owner should review event details before confirming and review the resulting event in Google Calendar.

Delivery and availability depend on Telegram, Google and the server. A network failure can leave a delivery or insertion result uncertain. The assistant avoids automatically repeating uncertain writes. It does not send invitations or emails on the owner's behalf through this integration.

Only connect mailboxes and calendar data the owner is authorized to use. Forwarding from another provider depends on that provider's settings and account rules.

See the privacy policy for data handling and the process for revoking access or requesting record removal.
